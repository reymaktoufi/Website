Offerings

What we can build together.

Workshops & Training

With more than 15 years of experience facilitating workshops globally, I have developed content for a variety of audiences and styles, including academia, nonprofit, and corporations. Through interactive, humorous, and engaging sessions, participants learn how to build trust, craft impactful messages and stories, and connect with audiences across formats.

I've led workshops and training programs for cohorts at HHMI, National Geographic, and Kavli Foundation, ranging from single sessions to multi-week cohort programs for early-career experts learning to talk about their work outside their own field.

Consulting & Strategy

I consult organiztaions on their communication strategy to help clarify messages and stories, design trust-based communication campaigns, and measure their impact.

Past strategy work includes social media-based communication campaigns for Tangled Bank Studios, Cloudberry, and Aspen Institute, helping each organization clarify its core message and reach audiences with evidence-based and data-driven guidance.

Speaking & Facilitation

I craft talks, keynotes, and facilitated sessions on the science of communication, misinformation, trust, curiosity, and public engagement in ways that bring the audience into the conversation.

I've spoken and facilitated sessions for organizations including the National Academies of Sciences and PBS, covering everything from the science of trust to the intersection of art and communication, tailored to audiences ranging from corporate professional and scientists to artists and storytellers.

Media & Content Consulting and Creation

As a content creator and strategist, I help organization by advising on podcasts, documentary, and educational content, from concept through production. In addition I contribute as host, narrator, illustrator, writer, and producer.

I have worked with a variety of media organizations including PBS|NOVA, Tangled Bank Studios, and SciShow in addition to individual National Geographic filmmakers, turning complicated topics into visual, story-driven formats with a focus on trust.
