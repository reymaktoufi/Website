[DRAFT — edit me] Dr. Reyhaneh (Rey) Maktoufi is an Iranian-American science communication trainer and strategist, and the founder of Talking Rhubarb Media. She holds a PhD in Media, Technology, and Society from Northwestern University, and works across workshops, podcasting, and documentary and educational content to help scientists and institutions communicate with clarity and warmth.

[DRAFT — edit me] A National Geographic Explorer and a 2025 delegate to the US–Japan Leadership Program, Rey has collaborated with HHMI, PBS | NOVA, The Story Collider, MIT, and the Kavli Foundation — bringing evidence-based communication training to teams who need their science to travel beyond the lab.
